Security Distribution & Pattern Heuristics

Risk Distribution
What this shows

Passwords are categorized by risk level. High Risk passwords are short (<8 chars) or from known breach lists. Medium Risk meet basic length but lack complexity. Low Risk are strong, enterprise-grade credentials.

Length Distribution
What this shows

Password length is the #1 predictor of crack resistance. <8 chars can be GPU-cracked in seconds. 8–11 chars need complexity to be safe. 12–15 chars meet NIST & PCI-DSS. 16+ chars are cryptographically robust.

Strength Breakdown
What this shows

The radar maps character diversity across the corpus. A strong profile scores high on all 5 axes. Lowercase near 100% is expected; the gaps in Special chars and Uppercase signal a major compliance failure and poor entropy.

Pattern Composition
What this shows

Identifies structural patterns that make passwords predictable. Numeric Suffix (e.g. password123) is the most exploited pattern in dictionary attacks. High Dictionary-based and Keyboard Walk rates indicate immediate remediation is required.
